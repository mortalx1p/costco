Frequently asked questions

Is there a cost to participate?

No. Participation is completely free. There are no fees, no subscriptions, and no hidden charges to join or earn credits.

How are partnered products selected?

Deals are curated based on member preferences and interests. The available offers are refreshed weekly to ensure variety and relevance.

Does Costco verify reviews?

Yes. Only Verified Purchasers who have completed a deal are eligible to submit feedback, ensuring all reviews are authentic and trustworthy.

Credits are automatically added to your Costco account after each completed deal is verified. No manual redemption steps required.
